---
title: General Meetings
---

General meetings must happen at least once per year and within six months after the end of the book year (**by December 31st**).

### Types of Meetings
- **Ordinary (Gewone):** Annual meeting to approve budgets, membership prices, and bookkeeping.
- **Extraordinary (Buitengewone):** Can be called anytime by Dragons or 1/5th of members.

### Announcement Procedure
- **Timing:** At least **15 days** ahead of time.
- **Channels:** Discord and/or Email.
- **Content:** Location, date, hour, and full agenda.
- **Member Input:** Members can submit topics up to 2 days before if signed by 1/20th of members.

### Proceedings
- **Quorum:** At least **1/2 of Dragons** must be present or represented.
- **Statute Changes:** At least **2/3 of Dragons** must be present or represented.
- **Voting:** Each Dragon has 1 vote. Chairman (Thane/Viceroy/etc.) is the tiebreaker.
- **Representation:** Must be delivered in writing to the chairman.

### Required Roles
Each meeting needs a Chairman and a Secretary (in order of hierarchy: Thane, Viceroy, Secretary, Treasurer, Eldest Dragon).
