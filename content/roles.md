title: Dragon Roles
---

Roles are responsibilities. You don't have to do everything yourself, but you must ensure it gets done.

### Exclusive Roles
The first four roles are exclusive and cannot be combined with each other.

#### Thane
The head of the organization. Responsible for planning, distributing tasks, and leading meetings.

#### Viceroy
Second in command. Assists the Thane and keeps them in check.

#### Secretary
Handles paperwork, digital documents, and meeting notes.

#### Treasurer
Responsible for bookkeeping, event budgets, and financial reporting.

---

### Other Roles

#### PR (Marketing)
Copywriting for all communications. Follows the [[announcements#2-day-rule|2-day Rule]].

#### Media (Marketing)
Formatting, graphics, and brand consistency. See [[branding|Branding]].

#### Dragon Resources (DR)
Our HR department. Minimizes drama and checks on mental health.

#### Discord Mod
Responsible for Discord organization, bots, and channels.

#### Scribe (Scriptor)
Takes notes during General Meetings.

#### General Venturer
Default role for standard meetups or when no specific Venturer is assigned.
