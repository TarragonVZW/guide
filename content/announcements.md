### Standard Schedule
- **Time:** Wednesday at 17:00.
- **Timing:** Special events must be announced **2 weeks** in advance.

### Procedure
1. **Drafting:** Drafts are located on the Google Drive.
2. **Approval:** Seek approval from **2 Dragons**.
3. **2-day Rule:** If a request for approval has not been rejected in **2 days**, it is considered approved.

### Requirements
- Announcements should always refer to the Discord event.
- **Legal Requirements:** Legal announcements must contain full VZW info (see [[legal-info|Legal Information]]).
- **Roles:** PR does copywriting, Media handles graphics.
