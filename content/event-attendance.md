### On-Site Arrival
- **Regular Events:** 15 minutes before.
- **Special Events:** 30 minutes before.
- **Communication:** Use the `#admin-channel` if you are running late.

### Commitments
- Try to be at **2/3rds** of all events.
- This responsibility is not applicable during vacations.
- Thane and Viceroy are by default general venturers.
