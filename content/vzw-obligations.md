---
title: VZW Obligations (What to do when)
---

This section covers the legal and administrative responsibilities of a Flemish VZW.

### Important Deadlines

| Date | Obligation |
| :--- | :--- |
| **March 31st** | **Patrimoniumtaks:** Tax on VZW assets (Exempt if assets < 50k EUR). |
| **June 30th** | **General Meeting:** Must be held within 6 months of the end of the book year. |
| **July 31st** | **Annual Accounts:** Must be filed within 30 days of the General Meeting. |
| **September 30th** | **Biztax:** Annual declaration for the legal entity tax (RPB). |
| **Annual** | **UBO Register:** Confirm current "beneficial owners" via MyMinFin. |

### Administrative Checklist

#### 1. UBO Register
- **Requirement:** Must be confirmed annually, even if no changes occur.
- **Changes:** Must be updated within **30 days** of any change (e.g., new board member).
- **Platform:** [MyMinFin](https://financien.belgium.be/nl/E-services/Ubo-register).

#### 2. Ledenregister (Register of Members)
- **Requirement:** A physical or digital register of all *effective* members must be kept at the VZW's seat.
- **Updates:** Changes must be recorded within 8 days.

#### 3. Belgian Official Gazette (Belgisch Staatsblad)
- Any change in the board (appointment/resignation) or statutes must be filed for publication within 30 days.

#### 4. Fiscale Verplichtingen
- **Rechtspersonenbelasting (RPB):** Even if there is no profit, a (nihil) declaration is mandatory via Biztax.
- **E-facturatie:** Be aware that B2B digital invoicing will become mandatory from **January 1st, 2026**.
